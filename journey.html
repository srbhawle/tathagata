Gautam Buddha (तथागत गौतम बुद्ध)

The Awakening & Foundational Sangha

Modern: Bihar & Uttar Pradesh (India), Lumbini (Nepal)
🏛️ Toponym Concordance: Ancient BCE ↔ Modern Pali / Contemporary
Ancient / ऐतिहासिक: Uruvelā, Isipatana, Rājagaha (Magadha)
Present / आधुनिक: Bodh Gayā, Sārnāth, Rajgir (India)

In the 6th century BCE, Siddhārtha Gautama attained Enlightenment beneath the Bodhi tree at Uruvelā (Bodh Gayā, Bihar). Delivering his First Sermon at Isipatana (Sārnāth, Varanasi), he set in motion the Wheel of the Dhamma.

Tradition: Early Buddhist Sangha (मूल संघ) Foundational
Epigraphic & Primary Fact / शिलालेखीय व ऐतिहासिक संदर्भ:

The First Council at Rajgir gathered 500 Arhats in Sattapanni Cave to chant and memorize the entire Vinaya and Sutta Pitaka orally.

Historical Anchor: Aśoka Rock Edict VIII; Piprahwa Relic Inscription; Cullavagga XI.
